# Example prompts

Drop these into your own Claude or ChatGPT once Webotee AI Connect is added. Swap the placeholders for the real brand, seller, category or ASIN you're researching.

## Sourcing & discovery
- "Find under‑competed brands under $50 in home & kitchen."
- "Top sourcing picks under $40 with rising demand, exclude gated."
- "Find under‑served niches in pet supplies between $20 and $70."
- "Is the `<niche>` category winnable for a new private‑label brand?"
- "Score ASIN `<B0…>` for FBA sourcing — velocity, gating risk, margin, ship speed."
- "Can I hit a 25% margin on ASIN `<B0…>` if my COGS is $8?"

## Brands
- "Evaluate `<brand>` for my portfolio — churn, FBA share, top ASINs."
- "Show me brands similar to `<brand>` I could source."
- "Which brands does seller `<X>` carry, ranked by estimated sales?"
- "Find single‑seller brands I could compete with."
- "Who's new on `<brand>`'s ASINs this month?"

## Sellers / operator network
- "Map the brands in `<brand>`'s seller network."
- "Compare seller `<A>` vs seller `<B>` — brand and ASIN overlap."
- "Which categories does seller `<X>` dominate?"
- "Show new sellers that started in the last 30 days with 5+ brands."

## Buy‑box & competition
- "Who's controlled the buy box on ASIN `<B0…>` over the past 24 months?"
- "Who controls the buy box in `<category>` right now?"
- "Is `<brand>` under attack — new sellers or price drops in the last 4 weeks?"

## Cross‑marketplace & protection
- "Compare ASIN `<B0…>` price on Amazon vs Walmart."
- "Show MAP violations on `<brand>` today."
- "Given my authorized sellers `[…]`, who's selling `<brand>` without authorization?"
- "Risk assessment for `<brand>` over the last 30 days."

## Monitoring
- "Track these ASINs: `<B0…>, <B0…>` and tell me what changes."
- "What changed on my `<brand>` watchlist since last week?"
